Good to Know

Frequently asked questions

What size kibble works with PETSDA feeders?
PETSDA feeders work with dry kibble between 0.2"–0.6" (5–15mm). Kibble within this range flows smoothly without jamming. Avoid irregularly shaped or oily treats that can clump.
How long does a 4L tank last?
A full 4L tank holds roughly 1.6–2 lbs (about 25–30 days of food for one cat on a typical daily ration). The sealed, desiccant-lined lid keeps kibble fresh the whole time.
What happens if the power goes out or WiFi disconnects?
Nothing is missed. Timer models run on 3 D-cell batteries as backup, and WiFi models keep their stored schedule and run on battery power — meals are dispensed as planned and sync once reconnected.
Can I feed two pets with different portions?
Yes — our Dual-Bowl models (D1, W2, C2) dispense into two stainless bowls, so each pet gets their own fresh portion at every meal. Portion size is set per feeding.
Is the camera model hard to set up?
Not at all. Install the PETSDA app, connect the feeder to your 2.4GHz WiFi, and you're live in under 5 minutes. HD streaming, night vision and 2-way audio work right away.
What's your warranty and return policy?
Every PETSDA feeder includes a 12-month warranty and is covered by Amazon's 30-day easy return policy. Our US-based support team responds within 24 hours.
