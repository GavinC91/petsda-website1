Privacy Policy

Your trust matters — here's exactly how we handle your data.

Last updated: October 2026

1. Who We Are

PETSDA ("we", "our", "us") designs and sells smart pet feeding products. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you visit petsda.com, use the PETSDA mobile app, or purchase our products on Amazon.

2. Information We Collect

  • Order information: purchases are completed on Amazon.com. We receive only order-related data necessary for warranty and support (e.g., order ID, product model).
  • App & device data: when you use a WiFi feeder with the PETSDA app, we process your account email, feeder schedule, feeding logs and camera stream access to deliver the service.
  • Usage data: basic analytics such as pages visited and device type, used to improve this website.
  • Contact data: if you email our support team, we keep your email address and message history to assist you.

3. How We Use Information

  • To provide, operate and improve our products and app features;
  • To provide customer support and honor the 12-month warranty;
  • To send service updates (e.g., low-food alerts, firmware notices);
  • To comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information. Camera video and audio are encrypted in transit and are not reviewed by us except to diagnose a technical issue you report, with your consent.

4. Data Sharing

We share data only with: (a) service providers who help us operate (cloud hosting, app infrastructure), bound by confidentiality obligations; (b) Amazon, to the extent needed to process your order and warranty claims; and (c) authorities where required by law.

5. Data Retention & Security

Feeding logs are retained while your account is active. You may request deletion at any time. We use industry-standard encryption (TLS) for data in transit and secure storage practices for data at rest.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your location (including rights under CCPA and GDPR), you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data, and may opt out of marketing communications at any time. Contact us at support@petsda.com and we will respond within 30 days.

7. Children's Privacy

Our products and website are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy occasionally. Material changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

9. Contact Us

Questions about this policy? Email support@petsda.com.
